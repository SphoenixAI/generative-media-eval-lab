# Independent source and scope check

Review date and publication cutoff: **2026-10-06**. Reviewer: media-research agent, checking the methods/standards and harness agents' selected claims against independently opened primary pages, then rechecking five volatile assertions from its own model survey. This is a second source review, not an independent reproduction of the papers' experiments. It does not establish exhaustive internet coverage, availability in a particular account, or findings from later in October.

## Correction found and sent to the author

**ITU-T P.910 freshness — corrected and rechecked.** The originally cited October 2023 edition is superseded. The official [current recommendation record](https://www.itu.int/ITU-T/recommendations/rec.aspx?lang=en&rec=16861) lists **P.910 (07/2026), approved 2026-07-29, in force**, and labels the 2023 edition superseded. The recommendation remains relevant to subjective video quality assessment, but its catalog does not prove which particular clauses changed. This was reported immediately to the implementation owner and standards researcher. The corrected report and source register now use the current edition and explicitly avoid a clause-by-clause conformance claim; both changes were rechecked locally.

## Methods, ontology and standards checks

- **Krippendorff method distinction — verified.** The author's [computing note](https://www.asc.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/2021-03/Computing%20Krippendorff%27s%20Alpha-Reliability.pdf) distinguishes ordinal from interval distance and gives approximately 0.743 nominal, 0.815 ordinal and 0.849 interval for its example. Its pairable-value treatment excludes lone observations from relevant marginals. The study supports reference fixtures; it does not validate this lab's rubric or observed human agreement.
- **NIST TEVV-Athlon status — verified with qualification.** The [NIST announcement](https://www.nist.gov/artificial-intelligence/ai-research/tevv-athlon-framework-evaluating-ai-systems) identifies NIST AI 200-2 as an **initial public draft**, announced August 7 with comments closing October 6, 2026. It addresses real-world AI-system outcomes, including multimodal and agentic systems. Treating it as a finalized standard would be incorrect.
- **ISO/IEC 25059 replacement — verified with qualification.** The [ISO catalog](https://www.iso.org/standard/80655.html) identifies the 2023 publication and a replacement FDIS under development. Neither a future replacement's final requirements nor certification of this implementation follows from the catalog.
- **C2PA provenance — verified with qualification.** The [2.4 specification](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/specs/C2PA_Specification.html) dates that edition to April 2026 and describes authenticated provenance assertions. Provenance authenticity is not a general factual-truth, physics-quality or copyright determination. No C2PA verification adapter was executed in this project.
- **PROV-O intent mapping — supported design inference.** [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) defines plans, roles, associations and delegation; a plan can represent steps intended to achieve goals. Plan representations and their goals are deliberately not prescribed. The lab's explicit intent, criteria and authority fields are application extensions, not a claim that PROV-O already supplies a complete creative-intent ontology.

## Agent-harness checks

- **Task-dependent multi-agent value — verified as reported.** The [scaling study, v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08296v3) reports 260 configurations, six benchmarks and three model families. Findings are conditional on architecture and task. They do not establish that thirteen media specialists beat a single competent judge.
- **Correlated consensus — verified as reported.** The opened [September 2026 full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.22512v1) evaluates error dependence, trusted-label calibration and different filtering rules. Rules help different dependence regimes; no universally superior voting rule is established. Its evaluated preference-pair setting does not directly validate video scoring.
- **Label-free judge weighting — verified with material limitation.** The [September 10 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12002v1) explicitly describes its shifting-distribution result as a simulation. Agreement-derived error estimates remain an experimental method for this lab; they cannot replace independent reference judgments.
- **Judge consensus versus human alignment — verified with scope limit.** The [revised geometry-of-judging paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03043v2), September 2, evaluates 42 judges on two Indic benchmarks. Its warning about shared subjective axes supports a local validity test, not transfer of its numerical results to generative media.
- **Harness distillation — verified as reported.** [Harness-Zero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24974v1), submitted September 21, studies training demonstrations and fine-tuning to transfer harness-induced behaviors. Keeping adapters replaceable is a reasonable inference. The paper does not remove application authorization, provenance or human-intent requirements.
- **Practitioner multi-agent experience — verified with scope limit.** [Anthropic's engineering account](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system) describes breadth-oriented research benefits, substantially greater token use and a successful single-judge grading configuration in that system. This is firsthand practice, not a media-evaluation controlled trial.
- **Behavioral plus outcome tests — verified.** The [Google engineering article](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/the-anatomy-of-harness-engineering-how-to-evaluate-iterate-and-guard-ai-coding-agents/) is dated September 9, 2026, and says behavioral checks complement end-to-end evaluation. A software test observing correct tool behavior does not prove a video judgment is accurate.
- **Caching experiment and date — verified after source clarification.** [Arize's article](https://arize.com/blog/prompt-caching-benchmark/) reports estimated costs, states that bills were not reconciled, and acknowledges model/provider-path confounding. The article shows October 2026; the publisher's [blog index](https://arize.com/blog/) supplies **October 2, 2026**. The date was independently checked after the harness author identified that primary index. Cache-hit rate alone is not a cost-effectiveness measure.
- **OpenTelemetry maturity — verified.** The inspected [GenAI conventions repository](https://github.com/open-telemetry/semantic-conventions-genai) still marks its schema URL TODO. Pinning a future export adapter is justified; the local typed trace is not a conformance claim.

1. **Sora 2 API is retired at this cutoff — verified from vendor record.** [OpenAI deprecations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations) lists September 24, 2026 shutdown for the Videos API and Sora 2 aliases/snapshots. Keep historical samples; do not propose it as a currently active generation API. No live endpoint probe was made.
2. **Nano Banana 2.1 release — verified from dated API changelog.** [Google's release notes](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/changelog) identify `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` as generally available on October 6, 2026. The October 29 predecessor shutdown is a future announced event. Vendor-reported improvements and local account access are not independently tested.
3. **Kling 4.0 rollout — verified and constrained.** The [September 30 announcement](https://kling.ai/blog/kling40-kling-visual-realism-creative-control-introducing-storytelling?tab=all) says early access with wider rollout coming in October, and specifies an eligible subscriber group for Flash. “Released” must not be expanded to universal API availability or every advertised feature being available now.
4. **LTX-2.5 weights — verified and constrained.** The official [model card](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5) supplies checkpoint instructions, a contact-information access gate and an LTX community license. This supports a gated weight-distribution candidate, not unrestricted open-source licensing, tested local execution or a verified performance ranking.
5. **Wan 3.0 API — verified and constrained.** [Alibaba's September 28 documentation](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/text-to-video-guide/) names `wan3.0-video` in working API examples and migration instructions. This establishes the documented product surface, not successful execution with this user's account, a weight release, or comparative quality.

## Result and unresolved validation

The selected primary sources support the conservative research conclusions after the P.910 freshness correction. No source establishes this project's production accuracy, human-time savings, construct validity, calibrated probabilities or universal best model. Source publication dates, revision dates, API snapshots and observed access dates must remain distinct.

The implementation was separately inspected for the synthesis. The strongest visible boundary is that the provider is a deterministic fixture simulator and the harness labels results as requiring human validation. Evidence coverage metadata, typed intent and referential checks are foundations; a metadata declaration does not prove actual media inspection or authenticated authority. The implementation owner's executed test report controls software readiness. This review provides no production approval and cannot substitute for real-media, held-out human validation.
