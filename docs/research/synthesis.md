# Research-to-implementation synthesis

Cutoff: **2026-10-06**. This synthesis compares the supplied master brief, the three research tracks, and the inspected Phase 4 Python implementation. Source verification is recorded in [the independent fact-check](independent-fact-check.md); source registers accompany [media methods](media-methods.md), [measurement and standards](methods-standards.md), and [the harness](agent-harness.md). The implementation's executed test report is authoritative for passing checks. No real-media evaluator, repair intervention or human study was executed by this research review.

## The useful task and competent baseline

The lab should help a reviewer decide **keep, repair, regenerate, or obtain more evidence** for a generated asset under a particular creative intention. Its value is fewer missed consequential defects and fewer human minutes to resolve a case. More agents, more graph connections, high agreement and elaborate explanations are intermediate properties, not evidence of that value.

The competent baseline is a trained human using the same explicit brief, dimension anchors and evidence log. Compare it with a single qualified multimodal judge preparing a review packet for that human, then with bounded specialists preparing the same packet. All conditions see equivalent media evidence and face the same task. Match both quality and resource budgets where possible; include repeated single-judge sampling at matched inference cost so extra tokens are not mistaken for the benefit of specialization.

An additional ontology ablation compares intent-bearing relations with a flat, searchable evidence log. Both retain the same observations and provenance. Measure whether relations improve decisions, reduce repeated investigation, expose contradictions or shorten handoffs. A graph that adds maintenance without measurable benefit should remain small.

## What the brief already established

The brief already required one backend and ontology; versioned prompts, models, rubrics and assessments; thirteen separate quality dimensions; a 0–4 anchored ordinal scale; critical failures that aesthetics cannot cancel; human evaluation and disagreement; provider abstractions; bounded specialist roles; hypothesis structures; leakage-aware presentation; and a deterministic backend demonstration. It also explicitly required stopping after Phase 4, before real providers, broad UI and subsequent research engines.

Research therefore strengthens a sound starting structure rather than claiming to invent these requirements. Its main changes are sharper measurement semantics, explicit intent authority, evidence coverage, distinction between software and empirical readiness, a current model lifecycle survey, and a falsifiable acceptance plan.

## Research changes visible in the implementation

**Intent controls applicability.** `IntentSpec` records objective, audience, context, constraints, per-dimension applicability, approval attribution and supersession. An agent-proposed intent cannot produce a passing overall verdict. A declared surreal-motion objective can exclude ordinary physical plausibility while retaining identity continuity. This is essential because a generic quality benchmark does not know what the creator intended.

**Relations are assertions with a reason.** `RelationClaim` stores typed endpoints, a specific predicate, evidence, asserting author, intent, purpose, scope, confidence, epistemic status and validity interval. Hypothesis relations cannot masquerade as observed facts. `Hypothesis` preserves alternatives, evidence for and against, prediction and falsifier. Repository checks prevent several dangling and cross-intent references. These records support the question “why does this relation matter to this goal?”; they do not infer a person's actual intention or establish causality.

**Absence remains visible.** A dimension result is scored, abstained or declared inapplicable. A scored result requires evidence and an integer anchor; inapplicability follows declared intent. Missing required dimensions produce `UNKNOWN`; a supported critical score yields a failure even when other scores are high. Confidence is explicitly uncalibrated. No ordinal average or unanimous vote grants production approval.

**Evidence records its observation boundary.** `Evidence` includes media identity, time interval, method, source, coverage and sampling timestamps. Repository checks compare times with asset duration and prevent assessment evidence from another output. This supplies the future capability gate: a still-image judge cannot support full-video continuity claims simply because its JSON is valid. At this checkpoint, recording declared coverage is not proof that frames were decoded or examined.

**Identity and history are stable.** Domain objects are strict frozen typed values with explicit revisions, canonical serialization and hashes. The repository persists versioned snapshots and references; SQLite prevents ordinary update/delete operations on core append-only tables. These checks expose incompatible snapshots and accidental mutation. They are not tamper-proof storage against an administrator who controls the database or machine.

**The swarm is an inspectable simulator.** `BoundedSwarm` accepts a finite set of dimension requests, pins the fixture-provider identity and evaluator version, bounds calls and time, validates outputs, preserves failures as abstentions and caches exact requests by provider/evaluator/request identity. It makes no live model calls and emits `HUMAN_VALIDATION_REQUIRED`. The deterministic fixture provider consults authored rules; it does not perceive a video. Role names do not make these outputs statistically independent. Adversarial, bias-calibration, regression and adjudication roles remain deferred.

**Agreement is an instrument, not a release badge.** The agreement module distinguishes exact agreement, Cohen's kappa and nominal/ordinal/interval alpha; ordinal alpha uses pooled category frequencies. Missing and degenerate data have explicit diagnostics. Results state that uncertainty has not been estimated. Human fixtures are authored records, not participants or evidence of rater reliability. Disagreement types are proposals requiring review.

**Presentation is a contract with a limited boundary.** The serializer assigns stable blinded A/B order, keeps tie separate from cannot-determine, and uses an allowlist for public/embed projections. Reveal follows a stored rating. These are backend contract functions: authenticated session binding, protected media delivery and a public service are later work. The FastAPI surface at this checkpoint is a health endpoint and a denied live-execution endpoint.

## What remains planned and must not be represented as implemented

- **Actual observation:** decoding, frame extraction, temporal-coverage enforcement, audio analysis, local metric execution, real VLM adapters, provider capability probes and verification that a cited observation occurs in the asset. Fixture evidence is authored text.
- **Empirical measurement:** real independent raters, calibration data, held-out accuracy, uncertainty intervals, noninferiority or regression tests, judge-family error correlation, real repair outcomes, cost telemetry and measured human-time savings.
- **Intent and authority expansion:** separate typed `Objective`, `Constraint`, `AuthorityGrant` and `DecisionRecord` artifacts; authenticated principals; executable limits and revocation; explicit mappings between distinct intents. Current textual constraints and approval attribution are not a general authorization service.
- **Formal interoperability:** PROV/JSON-LD export, SHACL validation, C2PA verification, pinned OpenTelemetry export and schema-migration tooling beyond the initial version. The application is informed by standards; conformance is not asserted.
- **Experiment governance:** a protected holdout store, exposure tracking, drift canaries, shadow promotion, rollback workflows, bias audits, causal intervention engine and gold-set amendment governance. Existing hashes and lineage fields are foundations, not the completed workflows.
- **Production operation:** authenticated API routes, browser rating UI, playable media, queue scheduling, enforceable paid-provider budgets, distributed concurrency and deployment. No deployed service or recurring monitoring is implied.

The source review also changed the candidate catalog: a retired API cannot be a default adapter, an early-access announcement is not universal availability, and gated weights are not unrestricted open source. Candidate judges with image input only must be evaluated under their sampled-frame limitation. These are adapter-selection constraints, not a ranking of measured local quality. Details and release evidence are in the media report.

## Measured acceptance plan

1. **Verify the offline control path first.** Run the required contract suite against the exact current code and record environment, suite and input versions. Include critical-veto and missing-evidence negatives, malformed/fabricated references, version mismatches, exhausted budgets, private-field leakage and an intentionally wrong fixture judge. Mutating an essential guard should make its corresponding test fail. Passing establishes an offline testing checkpoint only.
2. **Define a real pilot before choosing its winner.** An accountable human fixes intended use, applicable criteria, severe-defect definitions, decision consequences and feasible review/cost limits. Sample real media across relevant styles, motion types, generators and known difficult cases. Use a small pilot for instruction and timing problems, then determine the study size from baseline error frequency and a practically meaningful margin. No sample-size or efficiency claim is invented here.
3. **Freeze independent evaluation material.** Split by prompt family and media lineage, including derivatives and near duplicates. Keep development examples, repeatable canaries and locked audit material distinct. Record reviewer training and viewing conditions. Obtain independent ratings before discussion, preserve disagreement distributions and document adjudication. Repeated exposure to one clip must not count as an independent sample.
4. **Run the paired workflow comparison.** Counterbalance human-only, single-judge assistance and bounded specialist assistance to limit learning effects. Log active reviewer minutes, severe failures missed, false alarms, keep/repair/regenerate correctness, abstention and evidence coverage, overrides, latency, total inference/preprocessing cost and rework. Measure reliability per dimension and population; do not pool aesthetic preference with geometry validity.
5. **Decide with predeclared margins and uncertainty.** Prioritize the missed-severe-failure rate and correct decision outcome, then review time/cost. Compare paired cases with uncertainty respecting prompt/media clusters; document whether inference generalizes only to the observed raters. An inconclusive interval stays inconclusive. A system that appears faster by missing critical cases fails the usefulness objective. A single judge or flat log that performs equally well is a valid preferred result.
6. **Promote only the validated scope.** A new evaluator, rubric, extraction policy or model snapshot is a candidate version. Run contract probes, canaries and a bounded shadow comparison; retain the previous version for rollback. Recheck confidence calibration and subgroup performance when the data or model changes. Add or remove specialists according to measured marginal benefit.

## Sustainable human-agent handoff

Agents can assemble evidence manifests, detect missing information, prepare rubric suggestions, run authorized instruments, preserve contradictory observations and draft a concise decision packet. A useful packet names the unresolved decision, intent, criterion, media interval, competing explanation, next discriminating test and expected effort. The human supplies or confirms meaningful creative intentions, resolves consequential tradeoffs and approves scope changes to measurement or authority. Routine work can proceed inside an established authorization envelope without repeatedly asking for permission.

For a walking shot, an observation of apparent foot sliding can support alternatives involving camera motion, ground motion or a real contact failure. A relation records why each alternative matters to the intended realism. A proposed locked-camera comparison includes its prediction and falsifier. Until that comparison is authorized and observed, it remains a proposed test, not evidence that the cause was found or the shot repaired.

The durable core is the versioned intention, evidence, decision and authority record. Models, benchmarks and orchestration techniques are replaceable instruments around it. This architecture is intended to make change testable; sustainability and maximum efficiency remain empirical goals. **Stop at the Phase 4 offline checkpoint.** Subsequent live pilots and production features require a separately scoped continuation; no production-quality claim follows from an agent team's agreement.
