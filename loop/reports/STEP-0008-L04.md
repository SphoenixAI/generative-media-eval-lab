### Goal and relevance to NORTH_STAR.md

Implement L04 as a bounded local workflow for recording generation plans and family/arm declarations, registering PLANNED or FOUND clips, retaining selection and first-view history, and deriving intent provenance from stored evidence when a clip is bound. This supplies the generation-plan-to-clip link in `loop/NORTH_STAR.md:7-21` and makes the flagship slide family's declared camera arms auditable. ### Acceptance criteria

Copied verbatim from `loop/backlog.toml:86`:

> tests for the ordering rules, provenance degrading rather than failing, the append-only selection log, and FOUND clips.

The following sharpen that criterion and preserve all of the item's notes:

1. **Plans and families.** `GenerationPlan` retains every specified field: `family_id`, `arm`, `varied_factor`, `controlled_factors`, `prompt`, `model`, `model_version_string`, `settings`, `seed` (or null), `n_planned`, `intent_revision_ids`, `planned_at`, `notes`. `eval-pilot plan --file FILE` records human-supplied declarations and returns an immutable plan ID. Resolve each supplied intent revision to an exact reference and digest; never silently select the latest revision. Record `planned_at` from the application clock when recording the plan, not from a caller's backdated timestamp. Require a positive integer `n_planned`, a nullable integer seed, unique exact intent pins, and valid finite JSON settings. Unknown seed stays null. Family membership is the shared `family_id`; different arms retain their declarations without inventing a separate family registry or verifying that generation obeyed the declared controls. Test two TEST-ONLY camera arms, field round-trip, invalid input, and unchanged earlier plans after a new plan is recorded.
2. **Registration and binding.** `register --plan PLAN_ID` pins that stored plan and records PLANNED origin; registration without it records FOUND origin and remains valid with no plan, seed, model metadata, seal, or intent. Preserve `PilotClip`'s existing serialized shape by storing new origin/plan metadata in an additive artifact. Translate PLANNED to L02's existing `IntentBinding.origin="GENERATED"`; FOUND stays FOUND. A sole listed intent can be bound automatically for planned registration; multiple listed revisions require an explicit `--intent ID@REV` choice before media ingestion. An empty intent list leaves the clip unbound. Add `bind-intent CLIP --intent ID@REV [--prompt-only]` for an explicit later binding to an existing human-authored intent, including FOUND discovery after viewing. No binding creates or edits intent text. The new command reports the computed class separately from origin and leaves the quality verdict UNKNOWN.
3. **Ordering and degradation.** Use the L02 classifier unchanged: SEALED requires the exact revision's seal time < the time of a plan listing that revision < original registration time; otherwise CONTEMPORANEOUS requires that exact revision's seal strictly before the first logged view; otherwise RECONSTRUCTED. Legacy v1 remains RECONSTRUCTED; PROMPT_ONLY is an explicit exception for FOUND content bound to v2. Exercise strict orders, equalities, reversed orders, absent seal/plan/view, a seal for another revision, and a plan that does not list the chosen revision. A valid clip with insufficient chronology is admitted at the supported lower class. Missing optional evidence never becomes a fabricated event, a zero, or a pass. An explicitly requested missing plan, malformed input, wrong-kind reference, corrupt digest, or forbidden PROMPT_ONLY declaration is an integrity/input error, not a reason to pretend that no plan was requested.
4. **Binding history.** Populate chronology from stored plan, seal, registration and view artifacts, not caller-authored `SealEvidence`/`PlanEvidence` JSON or a provenance label. Choose the earliest qualifying stored seal for the exact intent pin, deterministically breaking equal-time ties by reference. Preserve L02's original registration anchor, predecessor pins and single lineage per byte identity. Retain the selected plan and view evidence in the private record closure. A revision cannot borrow another revision's seal, change origin/registration anchors, or raise an established provenance class. Later viewing or sealing never silently refreshes an old binding. Tests must distinguish missing chronology at initial binding (degrade and succeed) from an attempted later provenance promotion (reject and preserve history). Multiple intents in a plan do not authorize parallel binding lineages for the same clip.
5. **Selection log.** Add `eval-pilot selection DATASET --file FILE` to append a human-authored selection record containing `candidates_considered`, `rejected[]` with `ref` and nonblank `reason`, `random_draw`, and `kept`. Candidate references pin registered clips in that dataset; registration and retention in the selection log are distinct. Kept and rejected candidates must be unique, disjoint, and cover the declared candidate set, with no dangling references. Preserve the supplied candidate order. `random_draw` is nullable declared metadata, not an automatically performed draw or proof of random sampling. A correction appends the next revision with a digest-pinned immediate predecessor; it cannot overwrite the earlier selection. Test valid append/reopen/read-back, revision gaps, wrong predecessor/digest, duplicate or foreign candidates, blank rejection reasons, same-revision conflict, direct SQL UPDATE/DELETE refusal, and unchanged earlier record bytes. No code selects or rejects clips for the human.
6. **First-view log.** `open`, `open --at`, and `frames` share a first-view recording helper, anchored to validated source-byte identity so a new clip alias cannot reset the first view. Record the first eligible CLI access attempt after source verification and before extraction or viewer launch can expose content. Repeated calls and `open --at`'s internal frame extraction reuse the existing event. The first event is immutable, including when a later clock reading is earlier; admission/duplicate handling must be safe for two competing first calls. Missing/corrupt media or an invalid clip fails before recording a view. A later extraction/launcher failure retains an already recorded access attempt and must not be described as confirmed human viewing. Test both command paths, repeated calls, alias reuse, persistence across reopening, failure boundaries, and competing writers. Future `play` must use this helper, but `play` is not implemented in L04.
7. **FOUND and private audit coverage.** Exercise a plan-free FOUND clip through registration, viewing, explicit binding, selection logging, and private snapshot/export. Include unbound FOUND content, never-sealed intent, seal-before-view and view-before-seal cases, legacy intent, and explicit PROMPT_ONLY. An unbound clip has UNKNOWN intent evidence; it is not assigned a fabricated intent or an acceptability result. Newly created private snapshots include relevant origin/plan, selection, view and binding records with exact pins and their dependency closure; previously frozen snapshots retain their original bytes. Extend private `show` output to make these records inspectable. Public/embed serializers and accepted snapshot/schema artifacts remain unchanged.
8. **Limits and deterministic evidence.** Document that external viewing is not detectable by this CLI and that a logged access attempt does not prove a person watched. Local event ordering does not prove generation ordering. Model/version/settings/seed fields are declarations, not verified generator behavior or a regeneration guarantee. Use fixed inputs and controlled clocks to test reproducible classification, evidence selection, record serialization and frozen-export replay; do not call wall-clock times, fresh IDs, or future generated media deterministic. Retain the existing ingestion tool identities/hashes and record the application version for new lifecycle events. All executable examples and tests use TEST-ONLY text, generated patterns and explicit temporary roots.

### Method claims and evidence

External claims relied on, including inherited mechanisms, are individually extractable below. Primary documentation was inspected during planning; these citations establish method contracts, not that the proposed implementation passes them.

CLAIM L04-P8-M01: Pydantic v2's model_copy(update=...) creates a copied model without validating the supplied update values.

Source: [Pydantic 2.12 BaseModel.model_copy](https://docs.pydantic.dev/2.12/api/base_model/#pydantic.main.BaseModel.model_copy). Reuse `CheckedValue` and repository revalidation; test invalid copied nested values at admission.

CLAIM L04-P8-M02: SQLite permits one simultaneous write transaction, and BEGIN IMMEDIATE requests a write transaction immediately and may fail with SQLITE_BUSY when another writer is active.

Source: [SQLite transactions, sections 2.1-2.2](https://www.sqlite.org/lang_transaction.html). Perform new state-dependent admission checks within the existing writer transaction; do not infer whole-workflow atomicity from separate repository writes.

CLAIM L04-P8-M03: A SQLite trigger can terminate an UPDATE or DELETE statement with RAISE(ABORT, message), returning a constraint error.

Source: [SQLite CREATE TRIGGER, sections 2 and 6](https://www.sqlite.org/lang_createtrigger.html). Reuse the repository's existing append-only guards and verify them on the new log records. This does not prevent an administrator from replacing the database or removing its guards.

CLAIM L04-P8-M04: Python 3.12's standard-library hashlib provides a guaranteed sha256 constructor and hexadecimal digest output.

Source: [Python 3.12 hashlib](https://docs.python.org/3.12/library/hashlib.html). Reuse established artifact digests and media checksums for pins; introduce no hashing package or alternative digest contract.

CLAIM L04-P8-M05: RFC 8785 canonical JSON sorts object property names by UTF-16 code units and encodes the resulting serialization as UTF-8.

Source: [RFC 8785 sections 3.2.3-3.2.4](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8785.html#section-3.2.3). L04 consumes L03 `SealRecord` validation and its existing integer-only profile; it does not reimplement canonicalization, call ordinary artifact JSON JCS, or impose the seal's numeric profile on generation settings.

CLAIM L04-P8-M06: A CLI-only access log and local seal/plan/registration timestamps cannot establish the absence of earlier external viewing or that generation occurred after sealing.

This is a bounded inference from the proposed observation surface, also required by L02/L04. Checkable counterexamples are opening the source in an unrelated viewer before registration, or registering a previously generated clip after recording a new seal and plan. No external witness or system-wide viewing monitor exists in this scope. A viewer launch is recorded as an access attempt, not authenticated human attention.
