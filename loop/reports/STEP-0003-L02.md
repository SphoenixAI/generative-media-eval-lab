### Acceptance criteria copied verbatim from loop/backlog.toml

- Every derivation case is tested.
- A revision cannot borrow an earlier revision's seal.
- A revision without a reason is rejected.
- No edit can raise a record's provenance.

Sharper acceptance criteria, retaining every requirement above:

- V2 includes `use_context` with `surface`, `audience`, and `viewing_profile` (`FEED` or `STUDIO`); `criteria[]` with `id`, `dimension`, `priority` (`MUST`, `SHOULD`, `COULD` or `WONT`), `acceptance`, `rejection`, and `tolerance`; and `expected_deviations[]` with `dimension`, `description`, and `criterion_id`. Criterion IDs are unique within a revision; a deviation must reference a criterion in that revision with the same dimension. Multiple criteria may address one dimension. Required text rejects whitespace-only input, and tolerance remains human-authored text. No dimension is labeled `SENSITIVE` or `INVARIANT`.
- Every newly authored v2 revision, including revision 1, requires `revision_reason` from `SERENDIPITY`, `CLARIFICATION`, `CORRECTION` or `SCOPE_CHANGE`. Successors pin the immediate predecessor's exact reference and digest with the same identity. Missing, stale, skipped, cross-ID, cross-kind or digest-mismatched predecessors are rejected at repository admission as well as through the authoring interface.
- Derive `SEALED` only when the exact revision's seal time is strictly earlier than the time of its bound plan, the plan lists that exact revision, and the plan time is strictly earlier than this clip's original registration time. Otherwise derive `CONTEMPORANEOUS` only when that exact revision was sealed strictly before this clip's first logged view. Otherwise derive `RECONSTRUCTED`, including for never-sealed revisions. Equality never satisfies a strict ordering. A missing first-view event cannot establish contemporaneity and does not invalidate an independently satisfied sealed ordering.
- Seal evidence identifies the exact intent revision and its content digest. A prior revision's seal cannot qualify a successor. A plan for another revision or another registration cannot establish the sealed ordering. Absent or nonqualifying evidence falls through to the supported class; malformed evidence, invalid reference types, and digest tampering are rejected. Missing event times stay absent, never synthesized from the current clock or latest edit time.
- The only user-declared provenance is `PROMPT_ONLY`, allowed only for `FOUND` clips. A found clip without this declaration follows the ordinary derivation rules; an unrelated plan cannot make it sealed. A declared label cannot override computed provenance through constructors, copied models, serialized input or direct repository writes.
- Provenance is fixed on an immutable clip/intent binding snapshot. A permitted successor pins its predecessor and can preserve or lower the historical provenance ceiling, never raise it. Changed timestamps, a newly supplied seal, a new intent revision, a renamed binding ID or another alias for the same original registration cannot reset that ceiling. `PROMPT_ONLY` is not a numeric rank and cannot be an intermediate route to stronger provenance. A distinct registration has its own identity; editing a record cannot manufacture that distinction.
- Pilot 0 intents load through a read-only compatibility view as `RECONSTRUCTED`, preserving original references, canonical payloads and digests. Loading does not invent a revision reason, v2 context, criteria, or a seal, and does not persist a replacement legacy artifact.
- Persist and link the v2 intent and binding types, and include all relevant binding revisions and their pinned dependency closure in `PilotWorkspace.snapshot` and `export_snapshot`. A generated TEST-ONLY clip must travel through registration, v2 binding persistence, snapshot creation and export with exact references and digests intact. Later revisions must leave an earlier export unchanged; unrelated clips' bindings must be excluded. Existing snapshots and datasets with no v2 bindings retain their behavior.
- Derivation is not an assessment or verdict. Missing judgment evidence stays `UNKNOWN`. Documentation states what each provenance class records and cannot prove, including the inability of local timestamps alone to prove generation order, and I7's downstream restrictions on excusing deviations.

### Method claims and checkable local contracts

External claims are individually identified for deterministic extraction and research:

CLAIM L02-P3-M01: Pydantic v2 BaseModel.model_copy(update=...) does not validate supplied update values before returning the copied model.

Source: [Pydantic model_copy documentation](https://docs.pydantic.dev/2.12/api/base_model/#pydantic.main.BaseModel.model_copy), checked during planning on 2026-10-08. Consequence: test serialized revalidation at the repository boundary, including invalid nested copied values; frozen assignment alone is insufficient.

CLAIM L02-P3-M02: RFC 3161 defines time-stamping evidence that a datum existed before a particular time, requires a trustworthy time source, and does not examine the time-stamped imprint beyond its length.

Source: [RFC 3161, sections 1 and 2.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3161.html), checked during planning on 2026-10-08. This bounds the documentation's explanation of timestamp evidence; no RFC 3161 implementation or conformance is proposed.

CLAIM L02-P3-M03: A locally recorded seal-plan-registration ordering alone cannot prove that a clip was generated after its intent was sealed.

This is an inference from the distinction between recorded existence and the event being claimed, consistent with [RFC 3161's proof-of-existence scope](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3161.html#section-1), and a limitation expressly required by L02. Counterexample: an already generated clip can be registered after the seal and plan. A witness to intent existence alone also does not witness clip generation.

The following are repository facts, explicit local policies, or proposed verification targets, not externally validated methods:

- L02 in `loop/backlog.toml` defines the ordered provenance predicates, the four reason values, the two viewing profiles, and the four criterion priorities; these priority names make no requirements-standard conformance claim.
- `Artifact.canonical` and `Artifact.digest` in `src/eval_lab/domain.py` serialize and hash model fields, so adding serialized defaults to v1 can change its canonical bytes and digest; use a separate v2 type and preserve v1 serialization.
- `PilotWorkspace.intent` in `src/eval_lab/pilot.py` places the legacy free-text revision reason in `PilotSubmission`, while v1 `IntentSpec` has no such field; keep this legacy API compatible and require the enum on the explicit v2 contract.
- `Repository.put` in `src/eval_lab/persistence.py` revalidates serialized input, rejects conflicting existing revisions, and checks consecutive revision admission inside the write transaction; new binding uniqueness and provenance-ceiling checks must participate in that transaction.
- `PilotWorkspace.snapshot` currently roots datasets, submissions, sessions and derivatives; a new binding that only points toward existing media will not be found by traversing outward from that media. Add binding roots explicitly. The historical review's `L02-R1-01` in `loop/reports/STEP-0002/eval_r1.json` identifies this gap; that report is diagnostic evidence, not proof about this step's unbuilt code.
- Derivation will compare supplied timezone-aware event instants normalized to UTC and exact revision/digest identities, without consulting wall-clock time or floating latest references. Replaying identical evidence must produce an identical result, and equivalent timezone offsets must produce the same classification; tests must establish both properties.
- The complete weak-order oracle for three labeled times has thirteen cases: six strict permutations, six orders with exactly one tied pair, and one all-equal order. Cross that explicit oracle with first view before, equal to, after the seal, or absent, and with both clip origins; specify expected classes independently of the production function.
- Chronological classes use the local no-promotion relation `SEALED` to `CONTEMPORANEOUS` to `RECONSTRUCTED`, permitting equal or weaker successors only. `PROMPT_ONLY` stays separate and may stay prompt-only or degrade to reconstructed; reconstructed records cannot switch to prompt-only to escape their ceiling. These are admission policies, not numeric scores or probabilities.
- The frozen step configuration limits additions plus removals outside generated paths to 900 lines and excludes `loop/reports/**` from that count. The 840-line allocation below is a planning estimate, not a measured change count.
- This step introduces no new hash algorithm, canonicalization standard, calibrated quantity, empirical accuracy claim, or timestamp authentication guarantee. Existing digests pin repository payloads; L03 owns seal creation and verification, and L04 owns production binding/event collection.
