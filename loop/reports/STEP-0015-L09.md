### Goal and relevance to NORTH_STAR.md

Implement L09, Evidence roles, as a private, computed classification for an exact evidence/hypothesis pair. Apply the backlog's ordered TEST_RESULT, DISCOVERY and SUPPORTING rules using retained chronology, the competing-set revision pinned by the frozen test plan, and checked evidence-to-arm provenance. Nobody supplies the resulting role or its reason as authored input.

This supplies the evidence-role boundary between frozen test plan and result in `loop/NORTH_STAR.md`'s flagship case. A reviewer should be able to distinguish material used to form a hypothesis from a subsequent test of that hypothesis, while retaining the evidence and human judgment unchanged. This step does not resolve the flagship case or claim that a role proves causality, measurement validity or acceptability. `loop/state.json` currently marks dependencies L04 and L08 DONE; no historical evaluation score is reused as validation of L09.

### Acceptance criteria copied verbatim from the backlog

- A frame scrubbed after the hypothesis was created is SUPPORTING.
- A hypothesis added after the freeze never gets TEST_RESULT.
- Evidence from a post-freeze arm, created after the freeze, is TEST_RESULT.
- Evidence from an arm that already existed is SUPPORTING, with its reason.

Source: L09 in `loop/backlog.toml:158`.

### Sharper acceptance and implementation boundaries

1. **Pair-specific computation.** Accept exact evidence and hypothesis revisions, verify their retained digests and resolve explicitly pinned context. Compute a role for any requested pair, including evidence absent from the hypothesis's authored supporting/contradicting lists. Return the input pins, relevant plan/set/arm/source pins, a stable reason code and explanation, and the rule version. A role is not an evidence-wide property and is distinct from the existing human confidence-revision field named `evidence_role`. Do not alter that field or infer a role from it. For multiple retained arm links, check qualifying links in a defined stable order and identify the link establishing TEST_RESULT; never silently choose a latest plan or set revision.

2. **TEST_RESULT first, with all four requirements.** Require the queried hypothesis revision to predate `frozen_at` and to occur among the exact named members of the CompetingSet revision pinned by that frozen plan; require an explicit validated link from the evidence revision to an arm declared in that plan; require the evidence revision's `created_at` to be strictly after `frozen_at`; and require either the arm clip's original registration after `frozen_at` or a retained provenance record for that arm's instrument run made after `frozen_at`. Check the plan's existing freeze/dependency verification contract. A draft, an arm ID without a matching plan pin, a later set revision, a later hypothesis revision with the same ID, a residual member, or the string `source="instrument"` alone cannot satisfy these requirements. Independently falsify each requirement in tests.

3. **DISCOVERY second.** If TEST_RESULT does not apply, classify evidence already existing when the hypothesis was created as DISCOVERY. Also classify evidence cited by the exact observation explicitly recorded as prompting that hypothesis as DISCOVERY. Use a pinned TechnicalObservation and its existing evidence pins for that citation path; preserve the human-supplied prompting association in an additive context record rather than infer it from prose, confidence, a supports relation or shared media. An explicit prompting association must refer to an observation that existed by hypothesis creation. Check this route independently of the direct creation-time route, and include a fixture where DISCOVERY applies despite an old arm. The backlog's role precedence takes priority over a generic old-arm explanation.

4. **SUPPORTING third, with a reason.** With sufficient intact inputs to evaluate the preceding rules, everything else is SUPPORTING. Reasons distinguish evidence obtained later from an existing clip, a hypothesis absent from the frozen set, a hypothesis created at/after freeze, evidence not created after freeze, and no linked frozen test arm. The old-arm acceptance fixture uses evidence created after the hypothesis and after freeze, from a clip registered before freeze, so it exercises SUPPORTING rather than the earlier DISCOVERY branch. The scrub fixture uses a new evidence record attached to a frame obtained after hypothesis creation, with no qualifying post-freeze arm source. Reopening or extracting another frame from an old clip must not reset clip registration. Viewing an already existing evidence record later must not reset that evidence's creation time either.

5. **Chronology and UNKNOWN.** Use record-creation and original registration events, not a frame's media timecode, file modification time, context-import time, latest clip revision time or first-view time, to evaluate the backlog predicates. Strictly-before/after freeze excludes equality. If equal evidence/hypothesis timestamps leave creation order unestablished, preserve that uncertainty unless a retained prompting/citation path establishes DISCOVERY; do not invent a sequence from IDs or database iteration order. Distinguish a known absent optional arm/prompt link from a missing referenced artifact. Missing necessary records or unestablished chronology returns an explicit UNKNOWN status with no asserted role and an explanation; corrupt or mismatched pins returns INTEGRITY_FAILURE rather than a default role. A known failed TEST_RESULT predicate can still permit a proven DISCOVERY or SUPPORTING result. Missing first-view telemetry is disclosed and is not a new prerequisite for rules whose creation chronology is sufficient.

6. **Minimal append-only context.** Add a focused module for an evidence-to-arm link, a hypothesis-to-prompting-observation context, and the minimal retained instrument-run provenance needed by L09's alternative source rule. Links pin existing Evidence, Hypothesis, TestPlan, TechnicalObservation and PilotClip records as applicable; they carry no authored role, confidence, outcome, observation text or verdict. Validate arm membership, evidence/clip media identity and, when the arm declares a GenerationPlan, consistency with the clip's retained ClipOrigin plan. Preserve original registration through the ingestion pin; a later clip revision or newly imported link cannot make an old source new. Check retained same-content history so re-registering known old bytes cannot silently qualify as a new clip. Corrections append records with checked immediate predecessor pins and reasons, retaining all previous contexts.

7. **Instrument boundary.** There is currently no retained InstrumentRun type. Supply only a small private provenance record identifying an already performed run, its arm/plan, resulting evidence, execution timestamp and declared tool/version/content hashes. A link must resolve that retained record and check consistency with the INSTRUMENT measurement declaration and evidence; a free-form timestamp or source label in a role query is insufficient. Keep imported execution time distinct from application-recorded import time and reject impossible ordering such as a run after its purported result evidence. This is a declared local provenance record, not authenticated execution or a claim of instrument validity. Tests supply TEST-ONLY run metadata. Do not run an instrument, implement measurements, author a test outcome or build a validation/adapter framework.

8. **Private workflow and replay.** Provide explicit private context-import commands and a read-only exact-pair role query in the existing CLI, with schema/help for the additive types. Imports accept complete human-supplied associations/provenance and reject caller-supplied computed role/reason fields; ordinary role queries author no records and do not create a missing workspace. Include applicable context histories and all exact dependencies in new private dataset snapshots, including links whose evidence belongs to an arm clip and whose hypothesis belongs to the original case. Keep unrelated records out. Earlier snapshots and protected outputs remain unchanged; public/embed payloads contain none of the new records. Recomputing a role from the same pinned inputs, including a snapshot replay, must produce the same role, reasons and provenance references regardless of enumeration order.

1. Generative video evaluation and human evaluation protocols: [RAVEN-Eval: Rubric-Guided Automatic Evaluation for AI Video Generation Models Based on LMM Preference Judgement](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09111v1), preprint dated 2026-08-10. It evaluates task-specific pairwise rubrics and documents a five-expert, 50-task human study with ties, shared initial comparisons, agreement measurement, and adaptive scheduling. Relevance: concrete protocol and baseline candidates. Its sparse-frame judging has acknowledged temporal limitations; the results do not establish general evaluator validity.

2. Intent/context-conditioned evaluation: [RewardVerse: Rubric-Guided Policy Optimization for Video Reward Modeling](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.22947v2), revised 2026-09-23. It generates evaluation-query-dependent rubrics and reports reduced scalar-score drift. Individual rubric-theme scores lack direct calibration supervision, and the authors acknowledge remaining bias. Relevance: supports investigating context-specific criteria while keeping aggregate agreement separate from dimension-level validity.

3. Judge reliability and selective evaluation: [Certified Selective Automation of LLM Agent Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34320v1), preprint dated 2026-09-28. It studies correlated task groups, selective error, and task-disjoint calibration/testing. Its proposed bootstrap certificate is explicitly approximate and asymptotic, not a finite-sample theorem. Relevance: useful methodological evidence for auditing selective evaluation; transfer from agent trajectories to generative media remains unvalidated.

4. Provenance and annotation standards: [Content and Originator Authenticity Community Group](https://www.w3.org/community/coa-cg/), launched 2026-10-05. The group addresses interoperability of provenance metadata and originator identity, including C2PA and Verifiable Credentials.
