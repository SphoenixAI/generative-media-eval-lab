## Plan

### Goal and relevance to NORTH_STAR.md

Implement approved item L11, Relation v2, as an additive private backend contract for human-authored relations, including their discovery and exact revision retention in dataset snapshots. Store criterion anchors, separate epistemic and operational purposes, and authored warrant, qualifier and rebuttal without creating judgments or actions.

This serves the evidence-linked, reproducible and actionable evaluation thesis in `loop/NORTH_STAR.md`: the reviewer can follow an authored relation to its pinned intent and evidence, inspect its stated reasoning, and recover those same records from the flagship case's private snapshot. It supplies infrastructure for that case; it does not establish argument validity, media quality, resolution of a real case, or results from the intent study.

This is P2 planning only. This call fills this report's Plan section. Implementation, executable probes, tests and self-evaluation belong to P4; sealing, authoritative gates and disposition belong to the controller. The existing report skeleton and historical reports remain intact.

### Acceptance criteria copied verbatim from loop/backlog.toml

- tests.
- Stored v2 relations are included in private dataset snapshots, as roots or through incoming references, and a snapshot test with independently authored literals covers them.

Sharper criteria implementing the item's notes and the supplied prior findings:

1. Register an additive `RelationClaimV2` with `schema_version=2`. Preserve the existing relation information: subject, predicate, object, epistemic status, evidence references, author, free-text purpose, scope, validity interval and optional claim confidence. Preserve all existing predicates: `supports`, `contradicts`, `motivated_by`, `fulfills`, `violates`, `alternative_to`, `compatible_with` and `refines`. Add `creative_anchor`, `epistemic_purpose`, `operational_purpose`, `warrant`, `qualifier`, `rebuttal` and an exact predecessor pin. Do not change serialized v1 fields or v1 canonical payloads and hashes.
2. Newly authored v2 records explicitly supply `epistemic_purpose` from `SUPPORT`, `RULE_OUT`, `DISCRIMINATE`, `LOCALIZE`, `EXPLAIN`, `QUALIFY`, `SCOPE`, `OPERATIONALIZE`, and `operational_purpose` from `SHIP`, `REPAIR`, `REGENERATE`, `REVISE_RUBRIC`, `ADD_GOLD`, `RETRAIN_SIGNAL`, `INVESTIGATE`. Reject missing, null, blank, wrong-case, unknown and `UNSPECIFIED` values on this authoring path. The vocabularies are independent: do not infer purposes from predicates, require an unapproved pairing, or execute an operational purpose.
3. The relation's `intent` is a `Pin` identifying an exact retained `IntentSpecV2` or legacy `IntentSpec` revision and digest. `creative_anchor` is an ordered tuple of distinct, nonblank criterion IDs from that pinned v2 intent; an empty tuple means no anchors were supplied. Reject IDs found only in another intent or a newer revision, duplicate IDs, and mismatched intent digests. Legacy intents permit only empty anchors because their criteria have no criterion IDs. Never synthesize an ID from text, dimension or position.
4. `supports` and `contradicts` require a supplied, nonblank `warrant`; reject missing, null, empty and whitespace-only warrants. Other predicates may omit it. Optional `qualifier` and `rebuttal` remain absent when omitted and must be nonblank when supplied. Preserve supplied text, including meaningful surrounding whitespace, exactly. Keep free-text `purpose` separate; it cannot supply or overwrite the warrant or either purpose vocabulary.
5. Preserve endpoint-kind, evidence-kind, epistemic-status, timezone, interval and self-relation restrictions. Permit v2 intent endpoints for `fulfills` and `violates`, with the endpoint equal to the exact declared intent reference. Hypothesis endpoints must retain the same exact intent reference as the relation. Existing hypotheses remain legacy-intent records, so relations using those hypotheses pin that legacy intent and have no criterion anchors. Do not silently reinterpret a hypothesis under a different intent version. Relation labels and anchors are authored claims, not new technical observations, criterion assessments or deviation exceptions.
6. Validate references, nested copied/deserialized inputs, anchors and pin digests at repository admission, including an Evidence subject omitted from the separate evidence list. Preserve legacy evidence-to-intent validation. Evidence scoped to a v2 intent requires a retained `IntentBinding` with the same exact media reference and intent pin; neither manufacture a binding nor select the latest intent. Admission failure must identify the relevant contract and leave stored relation rows unchanged. Reuse transaction-local admission for retained pins and binding checks.
7. Revision 1 has no predecessor. Every successor pins the immediately preceding revision of the same relation kind and ID, including its digest. Reject missing, skipped, wrong-kind, wrong-ID and wrong-digest predecessors. Exact replay is idempotent; conflicting payloads at an existing revision fail. Earlier canonical payloads and hashes remain unchanged after reopening and successor insertion.
8. V2 `compatible_with` participates in the retained exclusive-competing-set constraint alongside v1. Test both endpoint orders, both insertion orders, retained historical sets, direct copied-object admission, and competing writers. Preserve the existing writer transaction boundary: an exclusive set and its conflicting relation cannot both commit. Do not introduce confidence arithmetic or modify hypothesis state.
9. Add an explicit `load_relation(repo, ref)` read entry point. A v2 reference returns its retained v2 record. A v1 reference returns a frozen, unregistered `LegacyRelationView` preserving source reference, canonical payload, digest and authored values, with both new purposes exposed as `UNSPECIFIED`, empty anchors and absent warrant, qualifier and rebuttal. Loading writes nothing and the view cannot be persisted as an automatic migration. Existing v1 repository reads, CLI and forms retain their contracts.
10. Include applicable stored v2 relation revisions in ordinary `PilotWorkspace.snapshot` and `export_snapshot` without requiring a v2 `PilotSubmission` envelope. Start from the dataset's existing roots and outgoing closure, then discover incoming v2 relations through exact subject, object or evidence references. A relation connected only through an included intent is eligible when its evidence media belongs to the dataset's retained clip/binding media context; sharing an intent ID alone must not import another clip's relations. Keep this selection scoped to the original dataset context, rather than recursively importing arbitrary incoming records through newly added dependencies. For each selected relation traverse all outgoing references, including its exact intent, endpoints, evidence and predecessor chain, and retain their digests using existing snapshot pins. Include retained applicable revisions, not only the latest revision. Existing dataset/media verification, snapshot ordering and seal inclusion remain in force.
11. Add an integration test that actually stores v2 relations, calls the ordinary private snapshot and export APIs, and checks literal relation references, digests, purposes, anchors and argument text plus exact dependency revisions. Use generated TEST-ONLY media in a temporary workspace, with no v2 submission envelope and no mock of relation discovery or export. Include an anchored v2-intent relation and legacy-intent support/contradiction cases with distinct purpose, warrant, qualifier and rebuttal literals. Author the expected relation payload independently of its constructor, `model_dump`, loader and exporter; derive its expected canonical bytes and digest from that literal oracle, not from the actual exported relation. Assert unrelated relations are excluded, including relations on another clip sharing the same intent and exact-reference mismatches. After appending a relation successor and a newer intent revision, a new snapshot retains the appropriate exact history, while the first export remains byte-for-byte unchanged under fixed export encoding, including after reopening.
12. Add TEST-ONLY positive and negative contract tests for all predicates and purpose values, required/optional text, exact pins and anchors, v1 compatibility, replay, revisions, exclusive sets and snapshot inclusion. Use independently authored, distinct field literals for storage and export expectations. Rejection tests use fresh identities and otherwise valid dependencies, assert specific failures, and verify no writes, so an unrelated revision conflict cannot mask a missing guard. Verify that relation storage leaves hypothesis confidence/status, absent evidence and UNKNOWN quality verdicts unchanged. With private sentinel relation text stored, public/embed payloads in blind and revealed states remain unchanged and contain none of that text. Prove fixed-input canonical serialization and hashing by tests; do not claim deterministic generated timestamps or workspace IDs.
