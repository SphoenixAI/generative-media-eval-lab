## 5. New items: the witness layer

### 5.1 What it is

Every judge, human or model, is treated as a witness.
1. **Blind statement.** The judge files a statement about a clip before seeing the intent, and the statement is sealed.
2. **Reveal.** The intent is revealed, and anything the judge changes afterwards is logged.
3. **Cross-examination.** Instruments check the parts of the statement that can be measured.
4. **Proficiency.** Blind items with a known, measured dose show what each judge can perceive at all.

Disagreements between judges are then classified as perception, suggestion, threshold or taste. Only taste reaches Sphoenix.

The method comes from forensic science:
- Linear Sequential Unmasking: record before seeing context, and log every later change.
- Blind proficiency testing: mock cases inserted unannounced into normal work.

No forensic data is used: no deception, emotion, identity or person-intent inference, and no law-enforcement or deposition footage.

Mapping to what exists:
- sealed intent and derived provenance (L02, L03) are the reference held back until the reveal;
- the view log (L04) records what a judge saw before the statement;
- TechnicalObservation and CriterionAssessment (L05) are the blind observation and the post-reveal assessment;
- Relation v2 warrants, qualifiers and rebuttals (L11) are each side's argument in a taste dispute;
- the "visible calibration/canary examples" in `docs/gold-sets.md` become WP04, with answers known by construction;
- "evidence grounding" and "reviewer minutes" in `docs/evaluator-validation.md` become measurable through WP03 and WP05.
